Bridges

Playing as

A daily bridge-building puzzle. Fresh islands every day.

Copied to clipboard!

Connect the islands with bridges so that every island has exactly as many bridges as its number, and all the islands form one connected group. Every puzzle is solvable by logic alone, no guessing.

The rules

  • Bridges run straight across or straight down, between two islands with nothing in between.
  • At most two bridges join any pair of islands.
  • Bridges never cross each other or pass over an island.
  • Every island ends up connected to every other one.

Controls

  • Tap an island to select it. Islands it can reach get a ring.
  • Then tap a ringed island to add a bridge between them. Do the same pair again for a double bridge, and once more to remove it. You can also drag from one island to another.
  • Tap a bridge to cycle it: single, double, none.
  • An island turns grey when it has all its bridges, and red if it has too many.
  • Tap Ansatz to explore a possibility. New bridges turn orange; choose Verified to keep them or Discard to return to where you started. After a discard, the first bridge you tried is highlighted for one move.
